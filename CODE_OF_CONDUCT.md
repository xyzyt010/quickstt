# Code of Conduct — Contributor Covenant

QuickSTT wants a practical, respectful home for voice-typing hackers on Windows and Linux Mint / Ubuntu / Debian.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of background, platform, or experience.

## Our standards

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, stay on-topic, help newcomers (especially Mint users new to AppImage/`.deb`).

**Unacceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, bad-faith disruption, doxxing, sexualized or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Report to `conduct@quickstt.com` (or any maintainer). We will review promptly, keep reporter confidential, and may moderate content.

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
