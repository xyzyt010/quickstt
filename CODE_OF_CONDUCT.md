# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want QuickSTT to be a collaborative, respectful, and practical project for contributors of all backgrounds.

Expected behavior:
- be respectful
- assume good intent
- give actionable feedback
- stay focused on making the project better

Unacceptable behavior:
- harassment or abuse
- personal attacks
- bad-faith disruption
- publishing private information without consent

Project maintainers may remove or moderate content that harms the health of the project community.
