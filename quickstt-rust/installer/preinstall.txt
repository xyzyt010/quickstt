QuickSTT — offline voice typing for Windows

Hold Ctrl+Space, speak, and your words appear where you were typing.
Runs entirely on this PC: no cloud, no account, nothing uploaded.

This installer will place QuickSTT in the folder below. Everything it
needs at runtime (speech engines and wake-word heads) is installed beside
the app.

Choose the installation type on the next page:
  • Full — app + all engine runtimes + wake-word heads + GPU library
    support (fastest when a GPU is present).
  • Compact — app + the CPU engine runtimes. Smaller download; a GPU only
    helps a few models, and everything still works.
  • Custom — pick exactly what you want.

Speech MODELS are not bundled: the app downloads them from Settings ->
Speech Models the first time you pick one, so you never pull gigabytes you
will not use.

The first launch opens a short onboarding tour (language, model, and how a
turn works). You can skip it at any time.
