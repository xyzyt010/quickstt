QuickSTT 2.0.0-alpha.19 — what you just installed

The pill: a small black bar that sits at the bottom of the screen.

Three ways to dictate
  1. Hold Ctrl+Space and speak — the text lands where you were typing.
  2. Hover the pill so it grows into the mic capsule, then click the mic.
  3. Say "hey jarvis" or "alexa" (wake words, optional and off by default).

Where things live
  • Speech models: Settings -> Speech Models (downloaded on demand).
  • Transcript history with audio: Settings -> History — play, copy,
    export audio, or delete everything.
  • Language: per model, Auto unless you force one.
  • Model recommendation: Settings -> Speech Models -> "Optimise for".

Nothing leaves this machine. Wake-word detection only loads while it is
switched on, so keeping it off costs no memory and no microphone.
